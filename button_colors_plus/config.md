Use **Tools → Button Colors…** to customize and preview your buttons.

Built-in presets: Classic, Ocean, Pastel, Colorblind friendly, Monochrome.
Save your own presets with **Save as…**. All settings, including saved preset
changes, are committed only when you press **Save**; Cancel discards them.

Colors use six-digit hex codes. Filled styles use white labels by default. Choose white, black, or automatic
contrast under Filled button text. White may be harder to read on pale colors. Outline/text styles use your selected color directly,
so check both theme previews for readability. Intervals appear above the buttons and use the theme text color by default.
Enable interval coloring to use the chosen palette colors in any style.

Settings are local to this Anki installation and are not synced by AnkiWeb.
